## Deliberate exclusions

- **Pirates source art** — a 1:1 reconstruction of a third-party game. The
  repository itself forbids generated replacement art. No image is bundled.
- **Fighter expanded roster** — contains caricatures of real public figures and
  has no documented right-of-publicity basis. Excluded. Only original
  compact-roster characters and active promotional art are included.
- **Bubbles Miku and Pepe cast** — third-party character associations; excluded
  from the internal portfolio library. Fizz and Bubblecap are the presentation
  mascots; Pippa is the owner's dog and is included as a shared-cast character.
- **Protocol 11 non-late-night artpacks** — no reliable licence/provenance
  record. Only the late-night set used in this release is included, still with its
  missing commercial-entitlement caveat.
- **Protocol 11 legacy brand JPGs** — pruned, no provenance record.
- **Launch Kit** — no approved brand mark; Night Signal remains a fictional
  sample, not a Launch Kit identity.
- **Private owner media, real signatures, credentials and unpublished creative
  references** — not read into the library.
- **`club`** — a specification pack, not an actively maintained deployed product.
- **`fishing-diary`** — a successor research/source repository for SHOALSHOT, not
  a separate maintained portfolio product.
- **Generated art** — no new fal.ai/OpenAI raster was added in this pass. The
  authorised network was unavailable during the run; no charge, upload or
  unapproved generation was attempted. Real project art and authored native
  compositions covered the library gaps. This is a recorded gap, not a claim that
  generation was exercised.

## Gaps and honest states

- LDraw has an original authored mark, favicon and install icons. The owner's
  taste approval of the final mark is not separately recorded; it is `current`,
  not `approved`.
- The LDraw social card is rendered from the real built app. The owner has not
  separately approved the composition as brand art.
- Most game/FM assets do not have a repository-wide redistribution licence. They
  are included for internal portfolio diagrams from the owner's public project
  repositories; that is not a redistribution licence.
- Real UI captures are dated and source-bound where available. They are
  screenshots, not artwork and not proof of every product capability.
- Architecture diagrams are reviewed from source but not owner-taste approved.
  Each record says so.
- Physical-device, stylus and screen-reader acceptance remain unverified.
