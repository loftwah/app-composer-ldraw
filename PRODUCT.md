# LDraw

## Platform

web

## Users and purpose

People and their authorised local agents draw, explain and export ideas using
the maintained Excalidraw editor. LDraw is independently deployable and stores
drawings on this browser only. No account, upload, collaboration or model call.

## Product authority

Composer issue #290, reviewed 2026/10/24. The L in LDraw references L from Death
Note; no third-party character artwork or affiliation is implied. Initial
project packs use actual approved public identities with local provenance.

## Boundaries

Local save, editable files and exports are different from cloud recovery.
Standalone and mounted origins do not share storage. Canonical editor entry is
the independent standalone app. Hostname/DNS/HTTPS and actual device support
remain unverified until separately exercised. Imported scenes are data and
never instructions, credentials or executable code.
